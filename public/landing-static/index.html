FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Do I need to link my bank account?
No. Duezo never asks for your bank login or financial credentials. You add bills yourself with Quick Add or Photo Scan. Your banking info stays with your bank.
Is Duezo free?
Duezo is free to download. The free plan lets you track up to 5 bills with the purple theme and basic reminders. Duezo Pro unlocks unlimited bills, custom reminders, all widgets, themes, calendar view, and more.
What does Duezo Pro cost?
Duezo Pro is $3.99/month or $19.99/year. The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial so you can try everything before paying.
Can I add bills manually?
Yes. Quick Add has autocomplete for 30+ common vendors. You can also snap a photo of any bill or statement and let AI extract the details automatically.
How do reminders work?
Duezo sends push notifications before bills are due. Free users get basic reminders. Pro users can customize timing — 7 days, 3 days, 1 day, or whatever works for you.
Does Duezo work on Android?
Duezo is currently iPhone only. It is built as a native iOS app with widgets and home screen integration.
Is my data private?
Yes. Duezo uses encryption and row-level security. Your data is never sold, shared, or used for ads. No bank credentials are ever collected.
